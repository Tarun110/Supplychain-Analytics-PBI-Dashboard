# 📦 Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end Supply Chain Analytics solution designed to provide actionable insights across inventory management, logistics, demand planning, and operational performance.

## 🚀 Project Overview

This dashboard enables organizations to monitor, analyze, and optimize critical supply chain operations through interactive visualizations and data-driven decision-making.

### Key Capabilities

📊 **Inventory Monitoring**

* Track inventory levels across warehouses and locations
* Identify stock shortages, overstock situations, and inventory trends

🚚 **Delivery Performance Analysis**

* Monitor shipment status and on-time delivery rates
* Detect delayed deliveries and logistics inefficiencies

⚠️ **Operational Bottleneck Detection**

* Identify process constraints impacting supply chain performance
* Enable proactive issue resolution before disruptions occur

📈 **Demand Forecasting**

* Analyze historical demand patterns and market trends
* Support accurate forecasting and inventory planning

💰 **Cost Optimization**

* Reduce excess inventory carrying costs
* Improve warehouse utilization and resource allocation

## 🎯 Business Impact

This solution helps organizations:

* Improve supply chain visibility
* Minimize stockouts and overstock situations
* Enhance delivery reliability
* Reduce operational costs
* Support data-driven strategic planning
* Increase overall supply chain efficiency
